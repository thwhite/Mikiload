# ⛏️ MIKILOAD

A birthday tribute to the flash-era classic mining game. Pilot your digging pod,
mine ever-rarer minerals, and haul them back to the surface to sell for upgrades —
a bigger fuel tank, a faster drill, a roomier cargo bay.

Dig deep enough and the ground may have something to say...

## Play

It's a single self-contained `index.html` — open it in any browser, or host it
anywhere static files can live (GitHub Pages works great). Playable on mobile
(touch controls) and desktop (arrow keys / WASD).

## Controls

- **◀ ▶** move / hold into a wall of dirt while grounded to drill sideways
- **🔥** thrust (watch your fuel!)
- **⛏** drill straight down
- Land on the surface buildings to refuel, sell ore, repair, and upgrade.

Progress auto-saves in your browser.
